Concept. The campaign aims to bring happiness to the Damac community through four pillars: wellness, family, investment and business. Together they lead to one goal, happiness.

Through talks, events and offers for existing and new clients, Damac is positioned as a human-focused brand that genuinely cares about its customers and community, rather than one that only talks about numbers and new projects. The approach lights the path to personal fulfilment, development and satisfaction, bringing the brand closer to its people.
